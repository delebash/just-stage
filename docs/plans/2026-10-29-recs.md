# JustStage — the recommendation list after the research (2026-10-29)

Written by the research lead (Opus 5.5) from RESEARCH.md §1–§13: round one and the second pass, ten areas, every
claim a rec rests on checked at its source by the lead (RESEARCH entries marked *web*). Each rec was argued against
before it was written; "Attack" says what the attack changed. Each piece is marked **standard** (with its doc) or
**NOT STANDARD** (with the standard option and the trade-off). The user's leans are context, never criteria
("dont let me influence your rec").

Hard requirements (TASKS, SCOPE): the whole human at CC5 and MetaHuman quality or better ("the best of the best";
reuse if it meets the bar, else build); CC5 + iClone + their plugins' features, sculpting, making clothes, props, the
pipeline out to Unreal, FX later; importing from other programs like CC5, Unreal and Daz; plugins and a user API with
standards before code; Windows, macOS and Linux; open source (MIT preferred, licence not limited); free — nothing
paid-only, no single-vendor core, no Omniverse, no Unity; built entirely by AI agents.

## Route and licence

**R1 · JustStage is a Blender fork, with a first-class open Unreal plugin.**
- Our own app, "JustStage", built from Blender 5.2 LTS. Every JustStage feature is written as bundled add-ons and
  an application template (Python, plus compiled modules where speed needs it), so it also runs on stock Blender
  and the fork's C/C++ diff stays thin. C/C++ changes only where Python can't reach: GPU skinning, new editor
  types (an iClone-style timeline), UI-toolkit changes.
- A JustStage plugin for Unreal (MIT) — the equivalent of CC5's Auto Setup + Live Link: imports JustStage characters
  on the UE5/MetaHuman-aligned skeleton with their DNA face rig, grooms and skin / eye / hair materials, and runs Live
  Link both ways. This is where MetaHuman-level real-time display comes from, as it does for CC5.
- Why (RESEARCH §6): Blender already has most of CC5 + iClone (sculpting, modelling, rigging, animation editors,
  node-based cloth and hair, EEVEE, Cycles, USD / glTF / Alembic / FBX), runs on every GPU vendor and all three
  OSes, and is the easiest base for AI agents (headless `-b --python`, `gpu.init()` in background, an official MCP
  server). Unreal has the best real-time humans, but its editor can't ship inside a product (EULA v15 and the merged
  EULA, read at Epic's CDN), a packaged Unreal app can't export FBX or USD at runtime, and users must install the
  engine (60–190 GB). Godot and our own engine leave the realistic human and nearly every tool to build; O3DE's
  macOS editor is experimental and it has no exporter; Flax can't host a fully open-source app.
- Standard: application templates are Blender's documented way to build "applications on top of Blender"
  (docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/advanced/app_templates.html). **NOT STANDARD: the fork** — the standard option is
  an app template on stock Blender; the fork buys our own app, GPU skinning and new editors, and costs a rebase on
  every Blender release (Bforartists and UPBGE do this). The user OK'd a fork (2026-10-29).
- Attack: (a) the Unreal plugin as the app — wins real-time quality and the iClone-side tools, loses on an open
  engine, install size, seat fees for companies over $1M (on users), weak sculpting and authoring for the CC5 side,
  and a slower agent build-test loop; (b) GPL instead of MIT (R2); (c) fork upkeep — kept low by the thin-diff rule
  and tracking LTS releases; (d) real-time quality below Unreal — covered by the plugin, Cycles beyond, and the gate
  (R3). **Changed:** the Unreal plugin went from "an export option" to first-class; the thin-diff rule was added.

**R2 · Licence: the JustStage app is GPL-3.0-or-later; its standalone libraries and the Unreal plugin are MIT.**
- Blender is "GNU GPL Version 2 or later" and published add-ons must be "shared under a GPL compliant license"
  (blender.org/about/license, read 2026-10-29), so the fork and its in-process plugins are GPL. Generators, the
  character schema tools and the Unreal plugin don't link Blender and stay MIT, reusable anywhere. External programs
  that talk to JustStage over its API are not plugins and may use any licence.
- Standard: the licence Blender sets. Attack: MIT preferred — not possible on Blender; keeping the libraries MIT
  keeps most of the value MIT. **Changed:** the library split was added.

## The quality bar

**R3 · The bar is a measured gate, before the features.** Build one JustStage head — base, generated skin, eyes,
lips, teeth, brows, lashes, scalp hair — and render it beside a CC5 HD character and a MetaHuman in EEVEE, Cycles and
Unreal (through the plugin) on the RTX 2070 Super. It passes when the user judges it at or above both. No source
compares any open asset with CC5 or MetaHuman (§5, §7), so only our own renders can show the bar is met.
- Attack: weeks before any feature work; but the bar is the main factor and building the app on a base that fails
  it would waste the rest. **Changed:** EEVEE was added to the gate, so JustStage's own viewport is judged too.

**R4 · Base human: MakeHuman's hm08 topology as subdivision level 0, two subdivision levels with displacement on
top, MakeHuman's morph space for the body, and Google's GNM head space for the face.**
- hm08 is all quads and made for subdivision (CC0; ~13.4k body vertices — CC3+'s body is 14,046 polygons); CC5 HD
  is the same idea, ×4 per level (70,732 and 282,928 polygons, §2). MPFB2's 1,258 CC0 targets cover infant to elder.
  GNM (Apache-2.0, scan-based, 253 identity and 383 expression parameters, eyes, teeth, tongue) is wrapped onto our
  head for scan-grounded faces. Anny (Apache-2.0) shows the MakeHuman data running outside Blender's code.
- Standard: reuse of open bases (§5); wrapping a morph space onto a topology is standard deformation transfer.
- Attack: our own topology from scratch — more control, years of morph work; MHR or GNM as the whole base — MHR has
  no eyes or mouth and is triangles, GNM is a head only; hm08's artist-made forms read stylised-realistic, so realism
  has to come from GNM's face space and generated detail — R3 judges it. **Changed:** GNM was added for the face.

**R5 · Character assets: reuse where it meets the bar, build the rest (§7, S10).**
- Build: the skin generator (physically based melanin and haemoglobin layers, procedural pores and wrinkles, general
  open image models for variation and 8K detail — Z-Image, Qwen-Image (2025-08), FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache-2.0),
  HiDream-O1 (MIT), all checked on Hugging Face and run by stable-diffusion.cpp on every GPU vendor); the eye
  generator (shader and procedural iris); lips, nails, wrinkle maps; the hair groom and card generator (Blender
  hair curves, Jolt's GPU strand-hair simulation, MakeHuman's seven CC0 styles as seeds).
- Reuse: OpenRigLogic (MIT, checked) for the face rig; GNM / ICT teeth and tongue geometry; Dem Bones,
  complementary dynamics and Jolt for deformation.
- The large scan datasets are non-commercial and mostly 1K: labelled, not a base. Every image-to-strand hair model is
  non-commercial or unlicensed and CUDA-only: labelled, not a base.
- Attack: training a skin model on non-commercial scans — barred by their licences, and they are 1K; buying scans —
  paid, out; procedural plus general models are unproven at scan quality — R3 decides. **Changed:** nothing beyond
  making R3 the judge.

## Platform

**R6 · AI engines: JustStage calls the family's engines (stable-diffusion.cpp, llama.cpp, audio.cpp) through the
web kit's runner as a local service, instead of starting its own copies.** The kit already installs them, pins
models and shares the 8 GB card between apps (§10); a viewport and an image model need that arbiter.
- Standard: our own existing code (the kit's `RuntimeService` and headless launcher, `just-core/docs/app-structure.md`
  §Q.3). Attack: a 3D app depending on a family Node service adds install weight; bundling sd.cpp directly would be a
  second way to do a job the kit already does. **Changed:** which kit server JustStage talks to is left to the
  standards spec — not settled here.

**R7 · Rendering: EEVEE in real time, Cycles offline (Apache-2.0, CPU / CUDA / OptiX / HIP / Metal / oneAPI), Unreal
through the plugin for MetaHuman-level real time.** Skin, eye and hair shading get fork work where R3 demands it.
- Standard: Blender's renderers. Attack: our own renderer — years; Unreal-only real time — the plugin already covers
  it. **Changed:** none.

**R8 · AI-agent testing: headless Blender (`-b --python`, `gpu.init()` in background since 5.2), golden-image render
tests (CPU Vulkan through Mesa's lavapipe on Linux and Windows CI; GitHub's `macos-14` runner for Metal), the MCP
control channel, screenshots for UI checks.**
- Standard: Blender's own render tests (`tests/python/modules/render_report.py`, oiiotool thresholds), wgpu's and
  Bevy's CI (§6). Attack: Metal has no CPU fallback — the hosted Mac runner covers it. **Changed:** none.

## Standards (the standards spec writes them out; these are the choices)

**R9 · Interchange: OpenUSD with UsdSkel for scenes and assets; glTF 2.0 + VRM 1.0 for engines; FBX in through ufbx
(MIT) and out through ufbx-write (MIT / Unlicense, work in progress) after round-trip tests into Unreal, Unity,
Maya and Blender; correctives and face rigs travel beside as companion files — MetaHuman DNA (OpenRigLogic) and our
own JSON schema.** No app's USD carries correctives today (S9); Daz has no USD export; Unreal's USD export is Beta.
- Standard: openusd.org, Khronos glTF 2.0, vrm.dev. Inside the app, Blender's own data is the model; USD is the
  exchange. Attack: USD as the internal model — Blender's data already is; FBX alone — loses correctives.
  **Changed:** the companion files were added.

**R10 · Skeleton: the UE5 / MetaHuman-aligned humanoid (CC5 aligned to it), with mapping tables to the open humanoid
names (VRM 1.0, Godot, Unity — converged), Mixamo, HumanIK and Daz Genesis 8/9 (Daz's bridge maps, Apache-2.0).
Face: a MetaHuman-style layered rig inside (DNA through OpenRigLogic), ARKit's 52 names for interchange, MediaPipe
(Apache-2.0) for webcam capture (51 of the 52).**
- Standard: Apple's ARKit blendshape list, vrm.dev, OpenRigLogic. Attack: VRM's skeleton alone — open and simpler,
  but CC5, Unreal and MetaHuman converged on Epic's. OpenRigLogic's trademark notice bars Epic's names in ours.
  **Changed:** the trademark line was added.

**R11 · Commands, API and plugins: every action is a Blender operator with a context-free `execute()`; one JSON Schema
per command; the same command reachable from the UI, Python, the command line (`-b`) and an MCP server on the
2026-07-28 spec (stateless, `server/discover`), built from Blender Lab's MCP server. In-process plugins are Blender
extensions; outside programs use the MCP / JSON-RPC API. Python execution over MCP sits behind a permission, as in
Cinema 4D's MCP; WebAssembly sandboxing waits (no DCC uses it; no threads, GPU or UI).**
- Standard: Blender's extensions manifest, the MCP specification. Attack: Blender Lab's MCP runs any Python
  "without any guards" — typed commands only, Python behind a permission. **Changed:** the permission was added.

## Product shape

**R12 · One app with workspaces — Character, Scene, Animate, Render & Export.** iClone users' worst seam is the
CC↔iClone round trip (an update bakes keys and wipes lip-sync, §3); engine editors and Blender use one scene with
workspaces. Standard: Blender's workspaces. Attack: two apps feel simpler — and recreate that seam. **Changed:** none.

**R13 · JustDirect stays beside JustStage, linked by the API.** JustDirect (web family) drives JustStage over MCP /
JSON-RPC and takes its USD / glTF scenes and render passes (depth, pose, masks) for AI video. A local video clip
takes 7.3 GB of the 8 GB card (§10), so it can't share the card with a live viewport; the two apps are on different
stacks; JustDirect's 3D stage is a small Three.js subset. Attack: one app was the user's lean (context) — merging
means rewriting JustDirect's UI inside Blender's or embedding a web view. **Changed:** none.

## Features without a ready open part

**R14 · Clothes: fitting first, making second — both built.** Fitting any garment to any body: built on LoBoFit's
method (BSD-3, SIGGRAPH 2026, a pending-patent notice) and Blender's cloth. Making from patterns: built, with ZOZO's
Contact Solver (Apache-2.0) for offline seam stitching on NVIDIA, AMD, Apple or CPU and Blender's XPBD cloth in real
time. Attack: no packaged open MetaTailor or Marvelous Designer exists; ZOZO's solver has never run on real AMD
hardware — test it; LoBoFit's patent notice is a label. **Changed:** the AMD test was added.

**R15 · Sculpting: Blender's sculpt mode (multires, dynamic topology, voxel remesh) as the base; ZBrush-level extras
(layers, morph brushes) added in the fork as needed.** No embeddable open sculpt library exists (§7). Attack: our own
sculpt engine — no. **Changed:** none.

**R16 · Animation and mocap: Blender's animation system plus our iClone-style timeline editor and GPU skinning in the
fork (PR #149020, open and unmerged, "Improves performance by 10-15x"); mocap through the VMC protocol (MIT) and
MediaPipe; Live Link through the Unreal plugin; auto-rigging with skin-tokens.cpp (Apache-2.0, CPU or Vulkan) and
Rigify; text-to-motion models offered with their licence labels (AMASS-trained ones are non-commercial) — the user
chooses, nothing filtered.** Attack: Unreal's Sequencer is stronger today — reachable through the plugin; nothing
open matches Cascadeur's physics tools — later. **Changed:** none.

## Order

**R17 · 0 the standards spec (the fresh Fable session; it also creates the 3D family's "one way per job" table) →
1 the quality gate (R3) → 2 the Character workspace (the CC5 side) and the Unreal plugin → 3 the Animate and Scene
workspaces (the iClone side: GPU skinning, the timeline, mocap, lip-sync) → 4 making clothes, sculpt extras, props →
5 FX and the JustDirect link.** A parity scorecard against CC5 and iClone, with a floor that only rises.

## Not recs — the user's to read or decide

- MetaHuman's terms read two ways: "any engine" (Epic, 2025-06-03) against the DNA Calibration licence's
  Unreal / Twinmotion limit (2022, never updated) — it matters only if JustStage reads or writes MetaHuman's own
  assets; OpenRigLogic's code is MIT either way.
- Epic's live EULA (seat terms for companies over $1M; updates 20 and 21) and the Fab EULA refuse scripted reads —
  the user's Epic account holds them.
