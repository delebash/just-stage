# JustStage — the recommendation list, version 2: character creation (2026-10-29)

Version 2 follows the user's word, 2026-10-29: "ok think on your recs again and go for character creation for now and
not iclone". Version 1 (all of CC5 + iClone, a Blender fork) is in git at `64f47a4`. Written by the research lead
(Opus 5.5) from RESEARCH.md §1–§13; every claim a rec rests on was checked at its source by the lead (RESEARCH
entries marked *web*). Each rec was argued against again for this scope; "Attack" says what the attack changed. Each
piece is **standard** (with its doc) or **NOT STANDARD**. The user's leans are context, never criteria.

Scope now: the character creator — what CC5 and its plugins do. iClone's side comes later; until then Unreal, reached
through the JustStage plugin, and Blender's own animation tools stand in for it.

Hard requirements (TASKS, SCOPE): the whole human — skin, eyes, lips, teeth, gums, tongue, every kind of hair, nails,
deformation — at CC5 and MetaHuman quality or better ("the best of the best"; reuse if it meets the bar, else build);
importing from other programs like CC5, Unreal and Daz; the pipeline out to Unreal and other software; sculpting,
making clothes, props; plugins and a user API, standards before code; Windows, macOS and Linux; open source; free —
nothing paid-only, no single-vendor core, no Omniverse, no Unity; built entirely by AI agents.

## Route and licence

**R1 · JustStage is a Blender extension and application template on stock Blender (5.2 LTS onward), plus an open
Unreal plugin. No fork now.**
- JustStage opens as its own application template: its own splash, startup layout, workspaces, menus, panels, themes
  and keymaps, with Blender's own panels replaced where they get in the way. Its features ship as Blender extensions
  (Python, plus compiled modules where speed needs it).
- The Unreal plugin (MIT) is the CC5 Auto Setup + Live Link equivalent (R12).
- Why Blender for a character creator (§6): its sculpting, modelling, UV, baking, hair curves, cloth, rigging, EEVEE and
  Cycles, and USD / glTF / Alembic / FBX are already there; it runs on every GPU vendor and all three OSes; it is the
  easiest base for AI agents. Unreal's editor can't ship inside a product and its sculpting is weak; Godot and our own
  engine leave the realistic human and nearly every tool to build; O3DE's macOS editor is experimental.
- Standard: application templates, "people can build their own *applications* on top of Blender that can be easily
  distributed" (docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/advanced/app_templates.html), and Blender's extensions platform.
- Attack: (a) our own app identity — a template gives our own splash, layout and workspaces; branding alone doesn't pay
  for rebasing a fork on every Blender release; (b) the fork's reasons were GPU skinning and a timeline editor — both
  iClone-side, out of scope now; the character side edits shapes on the base mesh (~13.4k vertices) with Blender's GPU
  subdivision on top; (c) the fork returns only if the quality gate (R3) needs viewport C work, or when an Animate
  workspace is built. **Changed:** the fork is dropped — nothing in the route is NOT STANDARD now (was: "a Blender
  fork", NOT STANDARD, in version 1).

**R2 · Licence: the JustStage extension is GPL-3.0-or-later; its standalone libraries and the Unreal plugin are MIT.**
- Published Blender add-ons must be "shared under a GPL compliant license" (blender.org/about/license, read
  2026-10-29). Generators, the character-schema tools and the Unreal plugin don't link Blender and stay MIT; outside
  programs that talk to JustStage over its API are not plugins and may use any licence.
- Standard: the licence Blender sets. Attack: MIT preferred — not possible inside Blender; the split keeps most of the
  value MIT. **Changed:** none since version 1.

## The quality bar

**R3 · The bar is a measured gate, before the features.** Build one JustStage head — base, generated skin, eyes, lips,
teeth, brows, lashes, scalp hair — and render it beside a CC5 HD character and a MetaHuman in EEVEE, Cycles and Unreal
(through a first, minimal plugin) on the RTX 2070 Super. It passes when the user judges it at or above both. No source
compares any open asset with CC5 or MetaHuman (§5, §7); only our renders can show it.
- Attack: weeks before features — but the bar is the main factor, and a failing base would waste everything after it.
  **Changed:** the minimal Unreal plugin moved into the gate.

**R4 · Base human: MakeHuman's hm08 topology as subdivision level 0, two subdivision levels with displacement on top
(CC5 HD's method), and three shape spaces wrapped onto it — MakeHuman's for range, Meta's MHR for scan-based bodies,
Google's GNM for scan-based heads.**
- hm08: all quads, made for subdivision, CC0, ~13.4k body vertices (CC3+'s body is 14,046 polygons; CC5 HD is ×4 per
  level, 70,732 and 282,928). MakeHuman's 1,258 CC0 targets cover infant to elder. MHR (Apache-2.0) is trained on 7,110
  body scans; SOMA-X (Apache-2.0) already puts MHR, Anny and SMPL on one rig and topology, and Anny (the MakeHuman data,
  CC0) outputs that topology — the bridge exists, and its CPU path is enough offline. GNM (Apache-2.0) is scan-based,
  with 253 identity and 383 expression parameters, eyes, teeth and tongue.
- Standard: reuse of open bases (§5); wrapping a shape space onto a topology is standard deformation transfer.
- Attack: our own topology — years of shape work; MHR or GNM alone — MHR has no eyes or mouth, is triangles and has no
  age range; GNM is a head; hm08's artist-made forms read stylised-realistic, so realism comes from MHR's and GNM's scan
  spaces plus generated detail — R3 judges. **Changed:** MHR added for bodies, through SOMA-X's existing bridge.

**R5 · Character assets: reuse where it meets the bar, build the rest (§7, S10).**
- Build: the skin generator (physically based melanin and haemoglobin layers, procedural pores and wrinkles, open image
  models for variation and 8K detail — Z-Image, Qwen-Image (2025-08), FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache-2.0), HiDream-O1 (MIT),
  checked on Hugging Face, all run by stable-diffusion.cpp on every GPU vendor); eyes (shader and procedural iris,
  wetness, occlusion); lips; nails; wrinkle maps; the hair groom and card generator (Blender hair curves, Jolt's GPU
  strand-hair simulation, MakeHuman's seven CC0 styles as seeds) for scalp hair, brows, lashes, beards, body hair and
  peach fuzz.
- Reuse: OpenRigLogic (MIT, checked) for the face rig; GNM / ICT teeth, gum and tongue geometry; Dem Bones,
  complementary dynamics and Jolt for deformation.
- Labelled, not used as a base: the large scan datasets (non-commercial, mostly 1K) and every image-to-strand hair model
  (non-commercial or unlicensed, CUDA-only).
- Attack: training on non-commercial scans — their licences bar it and they're 1K; buying scans — paid, out; procedural
  plus general models are unproven at scan quality — R3 decides. **Changed:** none since version 1.

## The character creator (CC5 parity — §2's inventory is the scorecard)

**R6 · One app with character workspaces: Body, Head & Face, Skin & Materials, Hair, Clothes, Rig & Expressions,
Import / Export.** Scene and Animate workspaces come later in the same app, not a second app — iClone users' worst seam
is the CC↔iClone round trip (§3). Standard: Blender's workspaces. Attack: a single big workspace like CC5's — CC5's
own panels split the same jobs. **Changed:** narrowed to the character workspaces.

**R7 · Sculpting: Blender's sculpt mode (multires, dynamic topology, voxel remesh), with shape-key authoring on the base
level so sculpted morphs become sliders — the ZBrush round trip happens inside the app.** ZBrush-level extras (layers,
morph brushes) are added as needed; a C-level sculpt need is a fork trigger (R1). No embeddable open sculpt library
exists (§7). Attack: our own sculpt engine — no. **Changed:** morph authoring made explicit.

**R8 · Clothes: fit-and-layer first (CC5's own model — conform, layers, tuck-in, hide-under, weight transfer), built
on LoBoFit's method (BSD-3, a pending-patent notice) and Blender's cloth; making from patterns later, with ZOZO's
Contact Solver (Apache-2.0; NVIDIA, AMD, Apple, CPU; offline).** No packaged open MetaTailor or Marvelous Designer
exists. Attack: ZOZO's solver has never run on real AMD hardware — test it before relying on it. **Changed:** fitting
first, matching CC5.

**R9 · Rig, expressions and auto-rig: our skeleton on the UE5 / MetaHuman alignment (R18); a MetaHuman-style layered
face rig (DNA through OpenRigLogic) with ARKit's 52 names for interchange; auto-rigging for imported characters with
skin-tokens.cpp (Apache-2.0, CPU or Vulkan) and Rigify; any humanoid mesh wrapped onto our base (CC5's Body Wrap);
posing and expression preview in the app.** Animation itself goes to Unreal or Blender's tools (R13). Attack:
UniRig's skin weights are CUDA-only — skin-tokens.cpp isn't. **Changed:** none.

**R10 · Photo-to-head (CC5's Headshot) and photo-to-body: built, late in the order.** No open option makes a textured
head on a production topology (§7); the open route is to fit a parametric head — GNM (Apache-2.0), or FLAME 2023 Open
(CC-BY-4.0) — to the photos, wrap it onto our base and project and complete the texture with the image models; SAM 3D
Body (SAM License, conditional) to MHR for bodies, which needs a CPU-offload fork to fit 8 GB. Attack: Headshot is a
paid add-on in CC5 and "best of the best" wants it; it rests on the base and generators first. **Changed:** placed.

**R11 · Import, like CC5, Unreal and Daz: FBX (ufbx), OBJ, glTF / GLB, USD, Alembic; characters from CC5 (FBX plus
its JSON sidecar — CC/iC Blender Tools, GPL-3.0, can be reused inside a GPL extension), Daz (its bridges, Apache-2.0;
Diffeomorphic, GPL, reads `.duf`), MetaHuman (DNA through OpenRigLogic) and VRM; any humanoid wrapped onto our base.**
The user decided importing CC models is fine (TASKS); CC's base mesh is never JustStage's base. Correctives travel in
companion files, not USD (S9). Attack: reusing GPL importers ties them to the GPL extension — which it already is.
**Changed:** the GPL reuse made explicit.

**R12 · Export and the Unreal plugin (MIT): one click to a set-up character in Unreal — skeleton, DNA face rig, grooms
(Unreal imports Alembic and USD BasisCurves grooms), skin / eye / hair materials on Unreal's shaders (MetaHuman's
texture override takes outside textures), Live Link both ways; updating a character in Unreal keeps its animation and
lip-sync. An optional "Send to Unreal as a MetaHuman" runs Epic's Mesh to MetaHuman in Unreal (Epic's cloud, MetaHuman
terms) for an exact MetaHuman. Plus FBX / USD / glTF / VRM out for Unity, Godot, Blender, Maya and the rest.**
- Standard: CC5's Auto Setup pattern; MIT Unreal plugins are common (303 tagged on GitHub). Attack: the
  MetaHuman option ties that path to Epic's cloud and terms — so it's optional and user-run. **Changed:** the
  keep-animation rule (iClone users' top complaint) and the MetaHuman option were added.

**R13 · iClone's side, for now: Unreal through the plugin (Sequencer, Control Rig, Live Link mocap, MetaHuman Animator —
Windows-only, under Epic's terms) and Blender's own animation tools. The standards spec still defines animation data
(clips, layers, timeline, USD animation), so an Animate workspace can come later without a redesign.** Attack: the
animation side rides on a closed engine — Blender's tools are the open path meanwhile. **Changed:** new in version 2.

## Platform

**R14 · AI engines: JustStage calls the family's engines (stable-diffusion.cpp, llama.cpp) through the web kit's runner
as a local service, sharing the family's model cache and its 8 GB arbiter, instead of starting its own copies.**
Standard: our own existing code (the kit's `RuntimeService` and headless launcher, `just-core/docs/app-structure.md`
§Q.3). Attack: installing a family Node service beside Blender is weight; bundling the engines would be a second model
cache and no arbiter. **Changed:** the shared cache made explicit; the exact service is the spec's to name.

**R15 · Rendering: EEVEE in real time and Cycles offline in the app; Unreal through the plugin for MetaHuman-level
real time.** Standard: Blender's renderers. Attack: our own renderer — years. **Changed:** none.

**R16 · AI-agent testing: headless Blender (`-b --python`, `gpu.init()` in background since 5.2), golden-image render
tests (Mesa's lavapipe on Linux and Windows CI; GitHub's `macos-14` runner for Metal), the MCP control channel,
screenshots for UI checks.** Standard: Blender's own render tests (`render_report.py`, oiiotool thresholds).
**Changed:** none.

## Standards (the standards spec writes them out)

**R17 · Interchange: OpenUSD (UsdSkel) for assets and scenes; glTF 2.0 + VRM 1.0 for engines; FBX in through ufbx and
out through ufbx-write after round-trip tests into Unreal, Unity, Maya and Blender; correctives and face rigs in
companion files — MetaHuman DNA and our own JSON schema.** Standard: openusd.org, Khronos glTF, vrm.dev. Attack: USD
alone loses correctives today (S9). **Changed:** none.

**R18 · Skeleton: the UE5 / MetaHuman-aligned humanoid (CC5 aligned to it), with mapping tables to VRM 1.0 / Godot /
Unity names, Mixamo, HumanIK and Daz Genesis 8/9 (Daz's bridge maps). Face: DNA + RigLogic inside, ARKit 52 for
interchange, MediaPipe (Apache-2.0) for webcam capture.** Standard: ARKit's blendshape list, vrm.dev, OpenRigLogic.
Attack: VRM's skeleton alone — simpler, but CC5, Unreal and MetaHuman converged on Epic's; OpenRigLogic's trademark
notice bars Epic's names in ours. **Changed:** none.

**R19 · Commands, API and plugins: every action a Blender operator with a context-free `execute()`; one JSON Schema per
command; the same command from the UI, Python, the command line (`-b`) and an MCP server on the 2026-07-28 spec, built
from Blender Lab's MCP server; Python over MCP behind a permission; in-process plugins are Blender extensions, outside
programs use the API.** Standard: Blender's extensions manifest, the MCP spec. Attack: Blender Lab's MCP runs any Python
"without any guards" — typed commands only, Python behind a permission. **Changed:** none.

## Product shape

**R20 · JustDirect stays beside JustStage, linked by the API** — it drives JustStage over MCP / JSON-RPC and takes its
USD / glTF scenes and render passes for AI video; a local video clip takes 7.3 GB of the 8 GB card, and the two apps
are on different stacks (§10). Attack: one app was the user's lean (context). **Changed:** none.

## Order

**R21 · 0 the standards spec (the fresh Fable session; it also creates the 3D family's "one way per job" table) →
1 the quality gate (R3), with the first Unreal plugin → 2 the base body and head (R4) and the skin, eye, mouth and hair
generators (R5) → 3 rig, expressions and auto-rig (R9) → 4 clothes fitting (R8) → 5 import and export, the full Unreal
plugin (R11, R12) → 6 sculpt extras (R7) → 7 photo-to-head (R10) → 8 making clothes from patterns (R8) → later: an
Animate workspace in JustStage, or staying on Unreal (R13).** The scorecard is CC5's inventory (§2), with a floor that
only rises. Attack: the plugin waits until step 5 — but the gate needs Unreal renders, so a first plugin comes at step
1. **Changed:** the order rewritten for character creation.

## Not recs — the user's to read or decide

- MetaHuman's terms read two ways: "any engine" (Epic, 2025-06-03) against the DNA Calibration licence's Unreal /
  Twinmotion limit (2022, never updated); it matters for "Send to Unreal as a MetaHuman" and for reading MetaHuman's own
  assets — OpenRigLogic's code is MIT either way.
- Epic's live EULA (seat terms for companies over $1M; updates 20 and 21) and the Fab EULA refuse scripted reads — the
  user's Epic account holds them.
