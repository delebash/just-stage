## 1 · The job and what we have

The app's eyes (`modules/head/part_materials.py`) are flat colours per GNM mesh region. The iris and the pupil follow
the mesh's polygons, so close up they show as a faceted ring (seen 2026-11-04, `eyenew.png`, in both engines).

The face test already shades the eye well (`tools/face_test/test_face_project.py`, rounds 19-38 and on):
- MPFB2's procedural eye (EnhancedEye: an iris of fibres, crypts and a limbal ring, and a white), from MPFB2's CC0 node
  data (`node_trees/procedural_eyes.json` at 6ff1eb35; RESEARCH ~2068), built by `tools/face_test/mpfb_nodes.py`.
- GNM's eye UVs laid out by the 3D radius (`_linear_iris_uvs`): GNM squeezes its pupil into UV radius 0-0.05 and its
  iris into 0.05-0.185, so a ringed iris landed warped.
- Settings learned over the rounds:
  - one eye per map, centred (0.5, 0.5), eyeball UV radius 0.46, iris 0.40 of it;
  - the second eye's centre moved off the map (the distance node sums both);
  - inner layer roughness 0.35, iris bump 0.1, outer layer IOR 1.15, no clear coat;
  - white (0.68, 0.60, 0.54) as the start;
  - pupil share 0.22 of the iris.
- What's the painting's (left in the test): the iris colours and the white calibrated from each painting.

## 2 · The rec, attacked

- **Move, don't copy (one way per job).**
  - The node builder goes to `core/node_trees.py`; the face test's `mpfb_nodes` re-exports it.
  - `_linear_iris_uvs` goes to `modules/head/eyes.py`, run when `head.add_parts` imports the eyes.
  - The EnhancedEye material goes on the iris, pupil and white faces; the cornea keeps its own glass and EEVEE layer.
  - The face test calls the same code, then sets its painted colours over it.
- **Data:** `procedural_eyes.json` through `tools/fetch_data.py`'s MPFB2 pin: the same repository, commit and CC0
  licence, one more include. It is not a new source.
- **Colours:** MPFB2's own defaults for a brown eye, the face test's learned settings above. An eye-colour setting
  (CC5's Eye tab, MetaHuman's iris presets) is the next step, not this one.
  - Attack: the face test's white (0.68, 0.60, 0.54) was calibrated against a lit painting. Kept as the start, since
    it is the measured value we have.
- **Check:** a test that the eye's iris faces get the EnhancedEye and the UVs run linearly by radius. Then close
  renders (EEVEE, Cycles) against today's faceted eye.

## 3 · Where things stand

DONE 2026-11-04 (tests: test_eyes 2; with test_head, the cornea test, test_lashes: 10 green).
- **Code moved.** `core/node_trees.py` holds the builder (the face test's `mpfb_nodes` re-exports it). In
  `modules/head/eyes.py`: `linear_iris_uvs` (in the eye's own space; the face test's `_linear_iris_uvs` calls it, and
  a second run changes nothing) and `shade` (EnhancedEye on the iris, pupil and white, each keeping its material and
  name). `parts.add` shades both eyes.
- **Data.** `procedural_eyes.json` through fetch_data's MPFB2 pin: one file, byte-identical to the face test's copy,
  which now reads the fetched one.
- **Colours.** MakeHuman's CC0 eye textures were measured first (iris ring, linear). Their "brown" is red-orange
  (0.08, 0.007, 0), as rounds 30-31 found, and "brownlight" amber. So the brown is round 70's matched painted iris
  (0.047, 0.023, 0.016): fibres 0.06 / 0.03 / 0.02 and 0.03 / 0.014 / 0.010, iris size 0.371, pupil 0.22 of it, white
  (0.68, 0.60, 0.54).
- **Seen** (`eyenew_mpfb.png` against `eyenew_faceted.png`, a fresh woman, close and three-quarter, EEVEE and
  Cycles): a round brown iris with radial fibres, a round pupil, a limbal ring and a clean highlight, in both engines.
  Before, it was a faceted polygon ring.
- **EEVEE's flat white: shadows, not an occlusion layer** (standard first: Blender's own settings before porting the
  face test's sheet, which needs ICT-FaceKit's mesh as a new source). Fast GI was already on. Trials on a close eye
  (`white_trial.png`, `white_jit.png`):
  - a finer shadow limit alone left the white flat;
  - full-resolution Fast GI changed little;
  - jittered soft shadows with a 0.1 mm limit drew the upper lid's shadow on the white, as Cycles does (harder there);
  - the cost: 0.56 s for a close eye and 0.44 s for a full body at every limit (the shadow maps refine only where the
    view needs).

  Built: the stage lights and the thumbnail light get `set_fine_shadows` (jitter, 0.1 mm), and the live view turns on
  Blender's viewport jitter (`use_shadow_jitter_viewport`). Shadows settle over a few frames once the view stops.
  Seen in the real window (`ui_eye.png`, the Average woman with lashes and long hair, framed on the face): brown
  irises, lashes along the lids, the whites shaded under the upper lid.
- **Open:**
  - EEVEE's lid shadow is harder than Cycles' gradient.
  - An eye-colour setting (CC5's Eye tab, MetaHuman's iris presets).
  - The face test's occlusion sheet, if the white still reads flat in close-ups.
