  older sclera darker, redder, yellower, age effect ~2x the sex effect (Kramer & Russell 2022, L*a*b*) [page];
  conjunctival melanosis in 92.5% of Black people (Singh 1998, cited) [page], 45.3% in a Mexican clinic sample [snip];
  pinguecula 0.7% under 20 -> 73% over 60 (Tehran) [API/snip]. Sclera colour by ethnicity: not yet measured (the 2025

## 4 · Children and teens - calibration (item 4; Appendix E)

Scope kept: measured studies, how makers do it, head norms, consent-stated close-ups by URL only. No image of a minor
was opened or downloaded.
- **Skin** [page/API]: cheek pores VISIA 0.61 at 3-7 y vs 8.51 at 19-23 (x14), texture 0.78 vs 4.46 (PMC7649423, 80
  Chinese); forearm furrow depth 33 vs 41 µm, principal direction 65° vs 50° (Corcuff 1983, 116 people 2-98); infant
  stratum corneum ~30% and epidermis ~20% thinner (Stamatas 2010); children lighter (higher L*) and less red (lower
  a*) than their parents (Fluhr 2000, values paywalled); melanin rises from birth to adolescence (800 Koreans);
  freckles from 2-3 y, x1.75 from 6 to 10; pores flat until ~20, a surge at 22 (1M Chinese selfies); scalp hair
  ~66 vs ~80 µm; follicle count fixed before birth (so density per cm² is higher in a smaller child).
- **Eyes**: cornea near adult size by ~2 y (EyeWiki) / visible iris 89% of adult at 4-5 y (South India); axial length
  21.8 mm at 3 y; interpupillary distance M 50.8 mm at 5 y, 56.6 at 10, 62.7 at 19 (MacLachlan formula) - **the eyeball
  and iris must not scale with the head; spacing and lids do**; bluish sclera normal in infants; iris colour stable by
  ~6 y.
- **How others do it**: CC has no child base (Reallusion staff: morph sliders, smooth the adult texture); third-party
  packs - "BB CC3 Child_Base Morph & Character Pack": "5 CC3 Child Base Characters plus 5 CC3 Morph Sliders to create
  Children characters of ages 1 to 18" [page, lead] (shape only); first-party "Face Young" and body-scale sliders; no
  child layer in SkinGen found. MetaHuman documents no child support. Daz: age morphs ("Growing Up for Genesis 9", 5 y
  to adult). **None documents a child skin - so the measured numbers above are the only target beyond "blur".**
- **MPFB2 / MakeHuman**: four age anchors only (1, 10, 25, 90 y), linear blends - a 15-year-old is 67% the 10-year
  shape; puberty is not modelled [code]. **Anny** (NAVER; Apache-2.0 code, CC0 data) calibrates the same targets to WHO
  growth standards - the one better-than-ours found for child proportions.
- **Head norms**: FaceBase 3D Facial Norms **summary statistics are open** - per-age tables (3-40 y; N, mean, SD) for
  each caliper and 3D measure, European-Caucasian, with a "Download Tables" link; e.g. male maximum cranial width
  140.22 mm at 3 y (n 9), 150.21 at 14 [page, lead]; terms UNREAD; individual 3D data controlled (DAC). Argentine body
  proportions 0-17 (sitting height / stature 0.67 -> 0.52; head circumference / stature 0.45 at 6 -> 0.34 at 17).
- **Consent-stated close-ups**: none free. Commons consent-tagged child close-ups: 0 [API]; Getty "Model released"
  listings ~$375 [snip]; pixel-shot (release claimed in a snippet, $1+) -> IMPORT / REFERENCE.
- Gaps: facial vellus density in children, normal children's lash length, child L*a*b* values in open text (§8).

### 2 · What this changes

**Sclera (rec 12).** Keep the procedural method - the only published, evaluated sclera-vein method (Bérard 2016) is
itself procedural growth with data-fitted profiles, and CC5 uses one texture plus a capillary normal. What to add, from
evidence:
- *Structure*: veins seeded at the fornix side and grown toward the limbus in three levels; a per-vein **depth** that
  makes deep veins bluer and blurrier and keeps thin veins shallow and red; a vessel relief in the sclera normal (CC5
  added the same). Patent label on Bérard's exact recipe; the physics is free to use.
- *Numbers*: quadrant redness nasal 2.3 > temporal 2.1 > inferior 1.7 > superior 1.6 (CCLRU), mean 1.9, abnormal > 2.6,
  men +0.2; generated vein masks should box-count near D ~1.6-1.7 (FSLB scale); age moves L* down and a*, b* up with
  effects ~2x sex; men redder and yellower.
- *Variety*: melanosis on by default for most Black characters (92.5%) and often Hispanic (45%), patchy and perilimbal;
  pinguecula as an age layer (rare < 20, majority after 60), nasal first.
- *References*: Wellcome's six Macroscopic Solutions photomacrographs (CC BY-NC, 2 cm across at 11811 px) are the
  sharpest real sclera we have - for calibration only. Photo texture sets stay IMPORT (3D.sk HD Eyes $4.99, Bauer).
  Still no shippable sharp sclera photo; still no measured sclera colour by ethnicity (the field says so itself).

**Nails (rec 14).** Keep the layered shader; nothing better was found. What to add:
- *Plate optics*: plate transmittance T = a·e^(-b·x) with b ~3.19 / 2.16 / 1.50 per mm (violet / green / red) - the
  plate filters blue more, so a thicker plate (toenails, free edge, age) turns yellow-grey by physics rather than a tint.
- *Melanonychia layer*: default probability by group (~80% African American, ~50% Hispanic, ~30% Japanese, ~100% Black
  50+), several nails and several grey-brown bands per nail, each < 3 mm (0.2-0.4 cm), more and wider with age;
  pigmented proximal fold on darker skin.
- *Lunula by finger* (both hands averaged): visible on thumb ~96%, index ~87%, middle ~82%, ring ~58%, little ~48%
  (adults 19-44, India); in the elderly mostly the thumbs.
- *Ageing*: longitudinal ridges in 85% of 60+, dull / pale plate 73%, grey 6%, great-toe thickening 23%.
- *Calibration set*: Yakimov 2024 (CC0, 18-95, fixed light and white reference) to fit plate colour against age.
- Sharp macro nail photos across skin tones: none shippable found in my scope (all (c), NC-ND or paid-no-alteration;
  CC BY OA case reports are low-res). The free-stock agent's results decide this part.

### 2 · What this changes for the gap

- **Skin is not "adult skin, blurred".** Measured, child cheek skin has almost no visible pores (VISIA 0.61 vs 8.51 at
  19-23 - x14), little texture (0.78 vs 4.46), a finer, denser polygon mesh of furrows and shallower furrows (33 vs 41 µm on
  the forearm). So the generator's child band needs its own fine-relief layer (rec 17's per-age band), not adult pores
  scaled down; pores become visible with puberty and surge in the early 20s (S8). CC5's own route, per Reallusion staff,
  is to smooth / blur the adult texture (C1) - the studies give a measured target beyond that.
- **Colour:** children are lighter and less red than their parents (S3), melanin rising from birth to adolescence (S10);
  freckles from 2-3 y, rising to the teens (S12, S13); thinner stratum corneum and epidermis (S2) argue for more
  transmitted blood colour per unit melanin - S3 measured higher perfusion but lower a*, so the a* target comes from S3
  (values paywalled - a gap until the full text is read).
- **Vellus / hair:** follicle count is fixed before birth (S14), so vellus density per cm² should scale up as the face
  gets smaller; scalp hair is finer (~66 vs 80 µm). No measured child facial vellus density or diameter was found.
- **Eyes:** the eye is near adult size early (cornea "by age 2", S20; HVID 89% at 4-5 y, S22; axial length 21.8 mm at
  3 y, S21) while eye spacing grows a lot (IPD ~50 mm at 5 y vs ~62 mm adult, S23) - so the eyeball and iris must not
  scale with the head; only spacing and lids do. Infant sclera can be bluish (first months), iris colour can darken until
  ~6 y (S17, S18). Lash length for normal children is unmeasured in what I found.
- **Shape check for MPFB2:** MakeHuman's age has only four anchor shapes (1, 10, 25, 90 y) and blends linearly between
  them (C15) - a 15-year-old is 2/3 of the 10-year-old target, so puberty (dimorphism from 6, male peak 12-15, nose 12-15,
  H3) is not modelled. Measurable checks: IPD by age (S23), sitting-height and head-circumference ratios (H8), WHO head
  circumference (H9), HVID (S22), FaceBase summary means and **average 3D faces by age and sex** (H1, open summary -
  page JS, needs the lead's browser). Anny already calibrates MPFB2's own targets to WHO growth standards (C16,
  Apache-2.0 / CC0) - the one "better than ours" for child body proportions found (rule f); its year mapping is not
  published.
- **How others do it (rule j):** CC5 has no child base (staff, C1; nothing in CC5 / ActorMIXER coverage, C9); children come
  from third-party morph packs (C6) and first-party "Face Young" + body scale sliders (C4); an age slider "using growth
  charts" was requested in 2019 and never answered (C3). MetaHuman has no child support in its docs and unanswered user
  questions (C10, C11). Daz uses age-morph products (5 y to adult, C13). None of them documents a child skin; shape is by
  morphs in all of them - the same as our MPFB2 route.
- **Data:** no shippable child scan or consent-stated close-up was found. The one child scan already on disk (D1,
  Renderpeople CC BY) is licence-clean but consent is unstated - a decision for the user, not a find to use quietly.
  Research-only child heads exist (Headspace / LYHM under-15 model, FaceBase 3DFN, SMIL / AGORA) for REFERENCE / TRAIN
  labels only.
