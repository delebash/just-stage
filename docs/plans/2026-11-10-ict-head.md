## 1. The design (OLD - superseded by §0)

### 1.1 What ICT-FaceKit's Light model is (read at its source, README at github.com/USC-ICT/ICT-FaceKit, 2026-11-10)

One mesh of 26,719 vertices / 26,384 polygons in parts by index range: #0 face [0:9408], #1 head and neck
[9409:11247], #2 mouth socket, #3/#4 eye sockets, #5 gums and tongue, #6 teeth (32 teeth, each its own range), #7/#8
eyeballs (sclera, iris), #9/#10 lacrimal fluid, #11/#12 eye blend, #13/#14 eye occlusion, #15/#16 eyelashes. **100
identity PCA modes** (linear morph targets over the whole geometry, from light-stage scans); **53 expression shapes**
in ARKit's names with _L / _R, and a FACS table (AU45 blink = eyeBlink_L + _R, AU12 = mouthSmile_L + _R, ...); a UV
layout (`figures/uvs.png`). MIT: "ICT-FaceKit is released under the MIT license"; the Full model (200+ modes, albedo)
is under a separate USC licence - NOT used.
