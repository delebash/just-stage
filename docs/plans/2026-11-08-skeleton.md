## The design (each piece's standard)

1. **Bones and parents**: the spec's §1.1 names; parents as Manny's (measured, the evidence file
   `docs/plans/2026-10-29-spec-evidence/ue5-manny-rest-pose.json`): twists children of their limb's main bone,
   metacarpals of `hand_*`, each finger's `_01` of its metacarpal, `thumb_01` of the hand, `clavicle_*` of `spine_05`,
   `neck_01` of `spine_05`. `breast_*` as MPFB2's `game_engine_with_breast` has it (head at joint-spine-1, parent
   `spine_02`; Manny has no breast). `Root` -> `root`. Standard: Epic's skeleton (the spec), MPFB2's rig.
2. **Joints**: MPFB2's rig JSON v110 and its placement (`entities/rig.py` `get_best_location_from_strategy` at the
   fetched commit: `CUBE` = the joint cube's mean, `MEAN` = the plain mean of listed vertices, repeats counted). The
   game-engine rig's 55 bones keep MPFB2's own joints, rolls and flags exactly. The 27 added:
   - twists at Manny's thirds as `MEAN` joints of the two joint cubes' 8 vertices each, repeated 2:1 / 1:2 (every
     joint cube has 8 vertices, so the mean is exactly 1/3 or 2/3 of the way); upper arm and thigh twist_01 at 1/3,
     _02 at 2/3; forearm and calf twist_01 at 2/3, _02 at 1/3; each twist bone runs to the next third (its limb's
     roll);
   - `spine_04` / `spine_05`: the game-engine rig leaves joint-spine-1 -> joint-neck without a bone (its `spine_03`
     ends at spine-1, `neck_01` starts at the neck) - the two new bones fill it: `spine_04` from spine-1, `spine_05`
     from a `MEAN` 3:5 of spine-1 : neck (0.625 of the way; Manny 0.62), to the neck; roll 0 as the spine;
   - `neck_02`: from the middle of neck -> head (`MEAN` 1:1; Manny 0.51); `neck_01` ends there;
   - metacarpals: MPFB2's default rig's own joints and rolls (`metacarpal1-4` -> index ... pinky).
3. **Weights**: MPFB2's CC0 `weights.game_engine_with_breast.json` kept for its 55 bones (the plan's §7 "Weights");
   the 27 added bones take shares of their source bone's weight (the plan's "seeded from `weights.default.json`"):
   - the hand's: `hand_*`'s weight split among the hand and the four metacarpals by MPFB2's default weights' shares of
     `wrist` : `metacarpal1-4` at each vertex (all to the hand where the default has none) - the default's own;
   - along a chain (upper arm, forearm, thigh, calf, spine_03 -> 04 -> 05, neck): the source bone's weight split by the
     vertex's place along the chain, linear between the bones' heads, flat past the last (a vertex at a twist bone's
     head is all that bone's; the twist each skin ring takes then grows linearly along the limb, as the twist bones'
     own fractions do) - **NOT STANDARD detail**: the standard option is the source rig's own twist weights
     (MakeHuman's default has its limbs in halves, not Manny's thirds) or Blender's automatic weights per new bone;
     trade-off: a smooth gradient set by position, not painted; measured at the gate's posed views;
   - at most 8 influences a vertex (the data model §2: "max 8 influences on the body"), renormalised;
   - base v2's appended lid vertices take their nearest hm08 vertex's weights (as the gate rig).
4. **No constraints or drivers on the twist bones**: the data model (§2 "Body correctives") gives twist-swing to
   `rig/body.dna`'s layer, "never hand-made drivers"; until it lands the twist bones move only when animated (an
   Unreal animation keys them) - the gate's twist view sets them by their fractions by hand.
5. **The product**: `tools/make_skeleton.py` writes `data/generated/rigs/ue5.json` and `weights.ue5.json` (made per
   machine from the fetched MPFB2 files, as every generated file; the spec's `data/rigs/ue5.json` path updated to it),
   `modules/rig/skeleton.py` builds and refits it on a character (an Armature object, a part of the character,
   bones in a `DEF` collection, the body its child with the Armature modifier first, the head's parts riding the
   `head` bone), the command `juststage.rig.add_skeleton`, the refit after every shape change, tests, the gate's
   posed views on it.

## Attack (passes)

- A, against the spec and the plan: the names, counts and parents are the spec's and Manny's (measured); the format
  is MPFB2's v110 with no new strategy (the 1/3 points are repeated-vertex means its own code computes); the 55 kept
  bones are MPFB2's exactly - nothing changes a user decision.
- B, against something simpler: MPFB2's 53 as is lacks spine_04/05, neck_02, twists and metacarpals - an Unreal
  retarget by name drops them; MPFB2's default rig's 163 bones aren't the spec's names or hierarchy.
- C, against the weights: the default's limb halves don't line up with Manny's thirds, so a twist bone can't take a
  default group whole; splitting by position keeps the game-engine total at every vertex, so the 55 bones deform
  exactly as before when the new bones aren't moved (testable: a pose on old bones only matches the gate rig's).
- D, the spine split: why not move spine_01-03's joints to Manny's proportions? The weights were painted for MPFB2's
  joints; moved joints would bend skin the weights don't follow. The new bones go where the game-engine rig has none.
- E, twists by constraint (Copy Rotation, the common Blender setup)? The data model gives twist-swing to body.dna and
  rules out hand-made drivers; constraints are the same job by another door. Nothing new in a further pass.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Don't change the user's approved Q-HAIR-7 or any approved text; don't `sed -i` a running script.
- Test with the user's characters (the library), not only `body.create`'s (eyes closed by default).
- The parts (eyes, teeth) are placed by `parts.refit` from the body at rest - check they still land right when the
  character is posed (a slider change while posed).
