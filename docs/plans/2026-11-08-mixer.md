5. **Tests, the gate** (a mix of one identity at strength 1 = that identity; seam-free borders), user docs (none exist yet

6. **The mix's skin (plan, mine, 2026-11-08; attacked below):** the approved "skins blend per detail layer" exists
   as the face test's `tools/face_test/skin_base.py compose` - pores and fine lines from ONE scan (`pores_first`), the
   blotchiness (`mid_from`) and regional colour (`broad_from`) from several by weight, the tone blended - which made
   the library's six scan skins (`tools/build_skins.py --composed`). The mix drives it: each identity's weight its
   share of the face (its values over the face's parts, by their mask mass), the pores from the largest; the rest of
   the recipe (age, mottle, freckles, moles) the character's. In the app: `compose` moves into the extension as a
   module (one copy - the face test then calls it), with OpenCV shipped as an extension wheel (Blender's documented
   `wheels` - as OpenRigLogic's; opencv-python-headless, Apache-2.0; OpenCV everywhere in `compose`: blurs,
   morphology, distance transforms, flood fill - a numpy port would rewrite it), the identities' split layers
   (`layers.npz`, 15 MB each) as generated data beside their shapes (LOCAL); a command `juststage.skin.from_mix` and a
   "Blend the skins" button in the panel (CC5 designates a skin apart from its Mixing mode; a 4K compose takes seconds,
   not a drag). Attack: (A) reuse - the composition and the bake already exist; extract them, no second blender; (B)
   cost - a wheel per platform (~40 MB), seconds a blend; (C) the likeness rule - >= 2 identities in the mid and broad
   bands, enforced by the command (one identity alone: refused, named); (D) the scans' licences before any ships.

7. **The likeness rule in the mixer (found 2026-11-08; the decision is the plan's §19.1 item 19, Q-SHAPE-1, taken
   under the go 11-07 - it stands):** "scan identities only through the mixer, >= 2 per region, and the recognition test
   on shapes too (a 0.99 / 0.01 mix passes '>= 2')". The mixer as built lets one identity fill a part alone. Fix (mine,
   attacked): a part's mix (each Blend-type part) holds no identity or two or more above 0 - `set_mix` (the wheel)
   refuses one alone, naming the rule; `set_slider` stays the raw per-value door (a package's sliders load through it
   one at a time, so it can't refuse the states between); the character is checked where it leaves the app - the
   library's save and the export refuse a part with one identity alone, naming it; the panel starts a part from picked
   identities (two or more, equal shares - CC5's wheel takes three or more targets before any mixing) and a share drag
   that would leave one alone is refused (the slider reads the character back). The
   "one identity at strength 1 = that identity" check stays a test of the mixer's maths (`mixer.set_value`), not a
   command path. The recognition test on shapes (a face recogniser on renders of mixes against each identity) and any
   cap on one identity's weight wait: the cap is the user's (`.dev/audit/2026-11-07/QUESTIONS.md` item 45). Attack:
   (A) it changes nothing approved - it builds an approved rule; (B) the gate's `mix*0` trial (one identity
   everywhere) becomes a maths-only check - the gate's mixes name two or more; (C) refusing in `set_slider` would block
   loading a package (its values arrive one at a time) - hence the checks at save and export; (D) a part can still be
   one identity at 0.99 -
   the recognition test is the guard for that, said plainly.
