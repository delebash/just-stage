## Design

The teeth part keeps its name, groups and riders; only its SOURCE changes - the way GNM's mouth came in:

1. **A tool places ICT's mouth in hm08's basis** (`tools/make_ict_teeth.py`, new): the Light model's #5 (gums,
   palate, tongue - one piece) and #6 (32 teeth) by the README's index table; placed by the front teeth onto the
   current part's front teeth at the basis (`ictteeth`'s placement, scale 1.0); written as
   `data/generated/ict/parts/teeth.obj` in MakeHuman's axes and decimetres (`bind_parts.read_obj`'s format) with
   `teeth.groups.json`: `lower_teeth_and_gums` (ICT's own jawOpen moves them rigidly - the share >= 0.5),
   `tongue` (#5 beyond 4 mm of any tooth on the jaw), `teeth`, `gums` (within 4 mm), for `part_materials`.
2. **`tools/bind_parts.py` binds it** to the same hm08 helpers as GNM's teeth (MHCLO, the head's span references) -
   so the part follows every slider by MakeHuman's own refit, and the skeleton rides the same groups
   (`skeleton.JAW_GROUPS`).
3. **`head/parts.py` reads the teeth from the ICT folder** (the eyes stay GNM's); `part_materials.PART_REGIONS["teeth"]`
   reads its regions from the groups (tongue, gums, teeth as today).

Standards: MakeHuman's MHCLO proxy binding (`core/mhclo.py`, MakeClothes' format - the way the current teeth come in);
the data from ICT-FaceKit (MIT, `ATTRIBUTION.md` row exists for the Light model).
