## 1 · What CC5 shows and what ours shows (seen 2026-11-04)

CC5 HD Aaron in its live view (SubD 1): scanned skin maps - pores, redness on the nose and cheeks, stubble, eye bags,
neck creases; the eyes wet, a pink caruncle and lower lid rim, a shadow under the upper lid on the white; a dark
blue-grey stage, a strong key with soft image light, real contrast.

Ours (`naked_*.png`, the library's Average woman on a new stage): one flat skin colour over the whole body (lips and
nipples the skin's colour), no brows, faceted ear and jaw outlines (no subdivision), a bright flat eye white, a pale
washed-out picture. A dark seam line beside the nose in Cycles; a bright blotch at the navel.

## 2 · Why (the causes, not the symptoms)

1. The app has no skin maps. CC5's realism is data: scanned colour, normal, roughness and micro-normal maps. Ours is
   the chromophore model's average colour - right on average, and realism lives in the variation.
2. The face test (`tools/face_test/`) already makes textured heads near this bar (`characters_four.png`: scan base +
   painted face, real pores and colour) - it was never moved into the app. I built panels and the library around a
   placeholder skin instead. Mistake not to repeat: the character's look before features (memory "quality-first").
3. The eye stack lacks CC5's layers: the occlusion film and the tearline (the face test had both, Cycles only).
4. The stage: three area lights with a strong fill, no image light, Blender's default grey world - flat and pale.

## 3 · The recs, attacked (what each attack changed)

1. **SubD 1 by default** (CC5's viewport reads "SubD 1"): the existing HD levels command at creation, 2 levels, the
   live view at 1. Attack: the export - the USD package exports at render evaluation; tests (export, lashes) pass with
   it. Attack: slider speed - Multires on the processor at level 1 (52k faces). Kept. DONE (build.create_human).
2. **The eye occlusion and tearline in the app** (`modules/head/eye_layers.py`, moved from the face test): ICT-FaceKit's
   strips (MIT, now fetched by `tools/fetch_data.py`), one object each for both eyes (`eyeshell`, `eyeEdge`, MetaHuman's
   names), placed by angle about each eye so a body change refits them. Attack: EEVEE drew the film but its darkening
   was too weak (tuned in Cycles, where the lids cast real shadows) -> CHANGED: the live view gets its own output with
   the same ranges darker (x2.5). Left: the lids' outline is found again only when the parts are added (a shape change
   that reshapes the lids keeps the old outline).
3. **The stage light** (next): an image light plus the key, a weaker fill, a dark stage colour, as CC5's view.
   Standard: Poly Haven's CC0 studio HDRI (already in `.dev/cache/hdri/studio_small_09_2k.hdr`, the face test's) moved
   into fetched data with its hash pinned. Attack: the world shows behind the character - the camera sees a plain dark
   colour (Blender's Light Path "Is Camera Ray"), the HDRI lights only. Attack: AgX's contrast look - measured before
   chosen.
4. **Skin maps in the app** (`modules/skin/maps.py`): the character's skin material reads a map set in the head's own
   tile (HeadTile, the face test's `_head_tile` moved into the app): albedo, normal (tangent space, baked from the face
   test's object-space map by Blender's bake), roughness, cavity, height; the body keeps the tone model. Attack:
   object-space normals break once the face moves (sliders, the rig) -> CHANGED: baked to tangent space once, offline.
5. **The library's characters from the face test** (man_a, amara, walter, mei - four people, each its own painting on a
   scan base): built by `tools/build_library.py`, licence labels from the scans (CC-BY, credited) and the painter.
6. **The body's skin**: MPFB2's CC0 region masks (lips, areolae, finger and toe nails, now fetched) colour those
   regions by the chromophore model (more blood, less melanin); the scan pore tile at real size everywhere (CC5 tiles
   a micro-normal too). Attack: MPFB2's own v2 procedural skin - its code would have to run (refused by Claude Code's
   permission check as code from outside, 2026-11-04); the masks are data and need no code.
7. **Brows on every new human** (strands, the hair module's MakeHuman CC0 grooms), and the eye white's live-view tone.
